# Week 5 — Economic, Legal, Technical and Financial Studies

**Previous:** [Week 4](04-examples.md) · **Next:** [Week 6](06-founding-and-location.md)

## Goals
- Carry out a feasibility study across all four dimensions.

## Key concepts
- **Feasibility study**: can this business work, before you commit money.
- **Economic**: market size, demand, competition.
- **Legal**: company type (şahıs, limited, anonim), permits, licences, intellectual property.
- **Technical**: technology, equipment, production capacity, suppliers.
- **Financial**: investment needed, working capital, break-even, expected return.
- A negative feasibility result is a successful study.
