# Week 2 — Classifying Entrepreneurship

> Syllabus (TR): *Girişimciliğin sınıflandırılması, avantajları ve dezavantajları*

**Previous:** [Week 1](01-what-is-entrepreneurship.md) · **Next:** [Week 3](03-opportunity-and-creativity.md)

## Goals
- Weigh the advantages and disadvantages of starting a business.

## Key concepts
- Classification by scale (micro, small, medium, large), sector, ownership, and growth ambition.
- **Advantages**: independence, unlimited upside, control, personal satisfaction.
- **Disadvantages**: income uncertainty, risk of losing invested capital, long hours, full responsibility.
- Why most new businesses fail: undercapitalization, no market need, poor management.
