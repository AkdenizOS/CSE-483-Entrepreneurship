## Goals
- Define entrepreneurship and name its main types.

## Key concepts
- **Entrepreneur**: someone who combines the factors of production to create value, bearing the risk.
- Entrepreneur vs. manager vs. investor — different roles, different risk.
- Types: opportunity-driven vs. necessity-driven; **intrapreneurship** (inside an existing firm); social entrepreneurship; technology entrepreneurship.
- Risk-taking, innovation and organization-building as the three functions.
