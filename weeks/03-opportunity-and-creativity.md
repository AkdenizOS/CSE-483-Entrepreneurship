## Goals
- Turn an observation into a testable business idea.

## Key concepts
- **Business idea** vs. **business opportunity** — an opportunity is an idea with a market.
- Sources of opportunity: unsolved problems, changing regulation, new technology, demographic shifts.
- **Creativity** (new ideas) vs. **innovation** (ideas turned into value).
- Idea generation: brainstorming, SCAMPER, design thinking.
- Screening: is there a customer, can you reach them, will they pay more than it costs?
