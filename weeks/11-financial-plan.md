# Week 11 — The Financial Plan

**Previous:** [Week 10](10-management-and-hr-plan.md) · **Next:** [Week 12](12-business-model.md)

## Goals
- Build the financial projections for a business plan.

## Key concepts
- Start-up costs and working capital.
- The three statements: income statement, balance sheet, **cash flow**.
- **Break-even analysis**: fixed costs / (price − variable cost per unit).
- Profit is not cash — a profitable business can still run out of money.
- Funding: own capital, family, bank loan, KOSGEB grant, angel investment, venture capital.
- Valuation basics and dilution.
