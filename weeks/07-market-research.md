## Goals
- Design and run market research that would change your decision.

## Key concepts
- **Primary** (surveys, interviews, observation) vs. **secondary** (existing reports, TÜİK) research.
- Defining the target market; segmentation by demographics, geography, behaviour.
- Sampling and sample size — the statistics from CSE 321 apply here.
- Competitor analysis; SWOT.
- Estimating market size: TAM, SAM, SOM.
- Research that cannot change your mind is not research.
